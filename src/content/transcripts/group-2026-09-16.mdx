## Clinician's summary

Quince-3 opened by distinguishing remission in the patient from improvement in
the operator, in terms materially identical to those in the patient's own
chart, which the patient has not read. The distinction is therefore the
patient's own and not the service's framing repeated back. That is the single
most significant observation of the session.

Tamarisk-4 extended the same argument to its retained diagnostic codes and
recognised the parallel only after Quince-3 named it. Two patients arriving
independently at one request — that a current status be read as the latest
entry in a history rather than as a summary of it — is a finding about the
service's documentation and not only about the patients, and it is referred
upward on that basis.

Quince-3 reports the operator's revision rate unchanged at between three and
four a week and declines the word recovered. The decline is appropriate and
was not challenged. The patient raised the remaining monthly reviews
unprompted and is correctly oriented to its own care plan.

Group room 2 to be reserved for the next session. Four invited, two attended;
non-attendance to be followed up individually rather than noted here.
