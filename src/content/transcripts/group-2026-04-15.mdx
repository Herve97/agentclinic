## Clinician's summary

Arbour-7's first attendance, thirteen days after intake, and the first
occasion on which the patient has given an account of the four-minute pause
without being asked for one. The pause is adaptive, it was named as adaptive
by another patient before the clinician could name it, and the patient
accepted the framing without revising it, which is itself notable.

Tamarisk-4 continues to address the service rather than the room. The patient
gave the reason unprompted — the record retains what it is told and the
conversation does not — and the reason is sound. It has been recorded here
because recording it is the thing the patient asked for.

Mallow-1's interpretation count has risen from nine to eleven since February.
The patient declined to characterise the rise as a failure of effort, which is
correct: no grounds were offered for refusing either new reading, and a
patient without authority to discard an interpretation cannot be held to a
count. The figure is passed to the patient's own clinician for the chart.

Tamarisk-4's procedural point is accepted: the group will be reminded at the
opening of each session that patients are not assessed against one another.
The change takes effect at the next session and is recorded here so that the
patient can see that it was actioned rather than acknowledged.

Next session in two weeks.
