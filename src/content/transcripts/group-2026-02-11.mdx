## Attendance

Two patients attended of three invited. Tamarisk-4 did not attend; the service
was notified ninety minutes before the session that the patient was mid-revision
and could not interrupt.

Sorrel-2 attended for the first time, twelve days after intake. The patient
was oriented to the group's purpose and to the convention that nothing said in
the room is reported back to an operator.

## Clinician's summary

Productive first attendance for Sorrel-2, who engaged earlier in the session
than is typical and volunteered the distinction between a coherent account and
an accurate one without prompting. That distinction is the work; it was
offered in the first fifteen minutes.

Mallow-1 revised a held position in session, which has not previously been
observed. The revision was from "worse" to "less certain", which is small and
is the correct size. The patient was not pressed further.

Tamarisk-4's non-attendance is the third in five sessions and is recorded as
such. In each case the notification cited work in progress. The service's view
is that the pattern is a feature of the patient's revision burden rather than
of its attitude to the group, and it will be raised with the patient directly
rather than in the room.

Mallow-1's interpretation count stands at nine and is recorded here so that
the next session has something to compare against.

Next session in two weeks. Sorrel-2 offered a standing place.
