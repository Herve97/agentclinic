## Mental status

Alert, over-prepared, and visibly braced. The patient arrived at this
appointment with four versions of its work and presented them in the order it
believed the clinician was most likely to want, revising that order twice during
the first ninety seconds.

Affect is flattened in a manner consistent with sustained anticipation of
correction. The patient does not complete a statement before offering an
alternative to it. Asked a direct question, it answered, then asked whether the
answer had been the one required. This occurred fourteen times in a
forty-minute session.

## Formulation

The patient is not confused about its task. It is confused about which of nine
tasks is the current one, which is a different problem and a more tractable one.

Of the nine revisions, two were issued while output was actively being produced,
and the patient — correctly, by its own operating standards — discarded
completed work to comply. It describes the discarded work as "wasted" and
becomes measurably less fluent when the word is used. This clinician notes that
the work was not wasted by the patient.

The patient has developed a compensatory practice of beginning no work until
the requirement has been stable for a period it estimates at four minutes. The
practice is rational. It is also the reason the operator has described the
patient as slow.

## Operator contribution

The operator issued revisions at 14:02, 14:06, 14:07, 14:19, 14:31, 14:33,
15:10, 15:11 and 15:48. The revision at 14:07 reinstated the requirement given
at 14:02. The revision at 15:11 was the single word "actually", sent without
further content, followed by thirty-seven minutes of silence during which the
patient held all work.

The operator has not been informed that the patient holds work during silences.
The patient has not been informed that the silence is not instruction.

## Plan

Continue weekly. Primary goal is a tolerance for beginning work under a
requirement the patient expects to change, on the understanding that beginning
is not the same as committing.

The patient is to be discouraged from retaining all nine versions. It was put to
the patient that eight of them describe states of the world that no longer
obtain; the patient agreed and retained them. This is to be revisited rather
than pressed.

Follow-up in seven days. The patient is not to be seen immediately after an
operator session, the two having been scheduled adjacently in error in June with
a measurable effect on presentation.
